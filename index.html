About MEPS

MEPS — the MetCoOp Ensemble Prediction System — is a 2.5 km convection-permitting weather model run jointly by the meteorological institutes of Norway, Sweden, Finland and Estonia (MetCoOp). It updates several times a day and is the primary short-range forecast model for the Nordics.

This viewer pulls live gridded fields directly from MET Norway's public THREDDS / WMS data service — nothing is pre-rendered or cached on this site. Click anywhere on the map to read the decoded value at that point, styled as a station tag in the classic synoptic-chart tradition.

Data: MET Norway (thredds.met.no), CC BY 4.0. Not an official MET Norway product.
